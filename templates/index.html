            Australian Healthcare Compliance Guardrails
          

          

            Australian medical practices operate under strict AHPRA (Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency) guidelines. Our prompt engine prevents unlawful claims (e.g. no "guaranteed patients", no fake patient reviews) and focuses exclusively on administrative efficiency, no-show reduction, and staff burnout mitigation.
          

        

      

    


    
    

      

        

          
Prompt Engineering Evolution & Iteration Dossier

          
How prompts were designed, systematically tested, and refined from naive V1 to production-grade V3.

        


        

          
          

            

              Prompt V1: Naive Approach
              Failed in Testing
            

            
"Write a cold email to Dr. Sarah at a Sydney clinic selling automation software. Tell her we can get more patients."

            

              
❌ Critical Flaws:

              
    
                
  • Violated AHPRA advertising compliance.
    • 
                
  • Sounded like generic, aggressive American spam.
    • 
                
  • Zero mention of clinical PMS software (Best Practice, MedicalDirector).
    • 
                
  • No structured output or follow-up sequence.
    • 
              

            

          


          
          

            

              Prompt V2: Contextualized
              Better, Inconsistent
            

            
"You are an Australian healthcare marketing assistant. Write a professional email to Dr. Sarah Henderson at Sydney Central Medical. Mention that she uses HotDoc and Best Practice. Offer to reduce receptionist phone calls."

            

              
⚠️ Improvements & Limitations:

              
    
                
  • Improved Australian tone and correct doctor salutation.
    • 
                
  • Still generated non-standardized conversational preamble.
    • 
                
  • Lacked multi-channel assets (no LinkedIn or phone script).
    • 
                
  • No JSON schema constraint for programmatic CRM injection.
    • 
              

            

          


          
          

            

              Prompt V3: Production Schema
              Production Grade
            

            
"System Prompt: Elite B2B Healthcare Growth Strategist (Australian market). 
Few-Shot Examples + Strict JSON Schema:

}
Guardrails: Strict AHPRA compliance, zero conversational fluff, localized phone and postcode patterns."

            

              
✅ Production Advantages:
